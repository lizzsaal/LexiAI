Privacy Policy for LexiAI - Grammar Checker
Last updated: October 3, 2026

LexiAI does not collect, store, sell, or transmit your personal data to the developer or any third party.

What the extension handles
- Text you type or select in web page text fields, so it can be checked for grammar and rewritten.
- Your settings (language, default tone, ignore list) and AI chat history.

How it is processed
All text is sent only to servers running on your own computer: LanguageTool (localhost:8081) and Ollama (localhost:11434). It is never sent to the developer, to Anthropic, or to any other external server.

Storage
Settings are saved using chrome.storage.sync, which Chrome may sync across your signed-in devices through your Google account. Chat history is saved in chrome.storage.local on your device only. You can clear chat history anytime with the "Clear chat" button, or remove all data by uninstalling the extension.

Third parties
LexiAI uses no analytics, advertising, or tracking, and shares no data with third parties.
